Overview

I work as a game designer on Star Stable, a live game with a regular rhythm of events and seasonal content. I conceptualize, prototype and implement gameplay, then test, bug fix and maintain the content I ship. I review designs with other crafts, track player feedback and KPIs, and document my work and learnings so the rest of the team can build on them.

Day to day I work in an agile team on live ops, which means designs have to be scoped realistically, estimated for time and risk, and presented to stakeholders with clear options and trade-offs.

Style Riders Show

Style Riders Show is a new multiplayer event that I drove from the first idea to release. A team of about five people delivered it in five weeks, and I owned my part of it end to end.

What I did

  • Wrote the original design documentation
  • Worked with server engineers to build the backend and database the event needed
  • Built the front end together with UI/UX
  • Helped QA test the new multiplayer system
  • Onboarded the people who implemented the event

Seasonal festivals

Seasonal content is a big part of the year on Star Stable. I pitch seasonal content projects to stakeholders and drive two yearly festivals. I took point on the Equestrian Festival and on the winter festival, covering the design from the first idea through to implementation alongside the rest of the team.

Festivals are built to come back. I design them as reactivatable content that can run again in later seasons, and that stays easy to maintain as a live service, so the effort of building one keeps paying off every time it returns.

Data-informed design

I use data to inform my design decisions rather than relying on instinct alone. I follow KPIs and player sentiment to see what is working and what isn't, use them to prioritize and adjust the content I own, and keep an eye on competitors for context. For economy design I build and balance in Excel with complex formulas, so changes to rewards and costs can be tested before they reach players.

How I work

  • Owning and scoping work together with the project lead
  • Estimating time and risk, and presenting options with pros and cons
  • Using KPIs and player sentiment to guide decisions, and keeping an eye on competitors
  • Onboarding colleagues onto the event work and onto our in-house tools
  • Presenting work to groups, and driving my own development plan with my manager

Tools

I work daily in Star Stable's proprietary PX Engine and PX Script, and I use Jira, Confluence and Excel a lot, including complex formulas for economy balancing.
